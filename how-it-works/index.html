How HerePup compares your health plan options

Open enrollment asks you to guess next year's medical costs. HerePup starts from what you actually spent: it takes last year's claims and works out what each plan you can choose would have charged, claim by claim.

Step by step

  1. Tell HerePup who's covered

    Add each person in your household and whether they're an adult or a child. HerePup uses this to pick the right coverage level for every option: employee only, employee plus spouse, employee plus children, or family.

  2. Add your plan options

    Drag in each employer's benefit guide or plan summary. Typed and scanned PDFs both work. HerePup reads the premiums for each coverage level, deductibles, out-of-pocket maximums, coinsurance, office, specialist, urgent care and ER copays, prescription tiers and employer HSA contributions. You check and confirm every number. If you and a spouse or partner have different employers, add both.

  3. Bring in last year's claims

    Drag in your explanations of benefits (EOBs), the claims spreadsheet from your insurer's member website, and your pharmacy claims. HerePup works out what kind of care each claim was, such as an office visit, a specialist, urgent care, the ER, a procedure or a prescription, and asks you to confirm anything it isn't sure about. Some insurers can also share claims directly.

  4. See what every option would cost

    Each claim is replayed through every plan and every way your household could enroll. You see which plan would have charged a flat copay and which the full deductible, when coinsurance and out-of-pocket maximums kick in, and the total: premiums plus your share of care, minus employer HSA money. When your files include what your insurer actually charged, HerePup checks its own math against it.

  5. See it month by month

    A low-deductible plan spreads its cost evenly through premiums; a high-deductible plan costs less up front but can bring big bills in the months you need care. The month-by-month view shows when each plan's costs would land, and how much to set aside for the expensive months.

  6. Spend less on the same care

    HerePup also looks across your claims for the same kind of care at different providers, such as two urgent care centers, the same test at two labs or the same drug at two pharmacies, and shows what you'd have saved at the lower-cost one.

HDHP or PPO? Why your own claims matter

Plans trade premiums against what you pay when you get care. A high-deductible health plan (HDHP) usually has the lowest premiums and often comes with employer HSA money, but you pay the full price of most care until the deductible is met. A PPO or EPO with a low deductible costs more every paycheck, but many visits cost only a flat copay.

Which one costs less depends on how your household uses care: how many visits, what kind, how many prescriptions, and whether one person or everyone meets the deductible. Averages and plan comparison charts can't tell you that. Your claims can, and they also show whether a heavier or lighter year would change the answer.

What you'll need

  • The benefit guide or plan summary from each employer offering you coverage
  • Last year's claims from your insurer's member website (EOB PDFs or a claims spreadsheet)
  • Pharmacy claims, if you fill prescriptions
  • About 10 minutes. Nothing is uploaded, and there's no account to create

What HerePup doesn't do yet

HerePup compares costs, not provider networks, so check that your doctors are in each plan's network. It doesn't model specialty drug tiers yet, and its results are estimates, not a promise of what a plan will cost. See the FAQ for more.
