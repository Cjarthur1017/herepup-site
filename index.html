Pick yoUr Plan

Choose your health plan with your own claims, not a guess.

HerePup runs last year's real claims through every plan your household can enroll in, across every employer, and shows which choice would have cost you least, once premiums, deductibles, copays and HSA money are counted.

Try HerePup in your browser

Drag in your benefit guides and claims. They're read on your device and never uploaded.

See how it works (2 minutes)

Shown with a made-up household and sample documents.

1

Add your plan options

Drag in each employer's benefit guide, typed or scanned. HerePup reads the plan options, premiums, deductibles and copays, and you confirm every number.

2

Bring in your claims

Drag in your explanations of benefits and the claim or pharmacy summaries you can download from your insurer's website.

3

See what each plan would cost

Each claim is replayed through every plan, so you see which would have charged a copay and which the full deductible. See month by month when the costs would land and how much to set aside, then test heavier or lighter years.

4

Spend less on the same care

See where your money went, and where the same kind of care cost less: one urgent care against another, the same test at two labs, the same drug at two pharmacies.

Your health data stays with you

HerePup runs on your own device, right in your browser. Your documents are read there and never uploaded. We don't have servers that receive them.

  • No HerePup account, and we never see your carrier password
  • Carrier access is read-only and only with your approval
  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising
  • Disconnect or delete everything at any time

Where HerePup is today

HerePup is in early testing, and you can try it now with your own documents. Its estimates come from your own documents and are not a guarantee of cost or a substitute for your plan's documents. Questions or partnership inquiries: chris@herepup.health.
