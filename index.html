How it works

1

Add your plan options

Drag in each employer's benefit guide, typed or scanned. HerePup reads the plan options, premiums, deductibles and copays, and you confirm every number.

2

Bring in your claims

Drag in your explanations of benefits and the claim or pharmacy summaries you can download from your insurer's website.

3

See what each plan would cost

Each claim is replayed through every plan, so you see which would have charged a copay and which the full deductible. See month by month when the costs would land and how much to set aside, then test heavier or lighter years.

4

Spend less on the same care

See where your money went, and where the same kind of care cost less: one urgent care against another, the same test at two labs, the same drug at two pharmacies.

Common questions

Do you see my health information?

No. HerePup runs in your web browser, on your device. Your benefit guides and claims are read there and never uploaded, and HerePup has no servers that receive them. The site only counts anonymous visits and a few steps, with no cookies. You can erase everything with "Delete all my data" in the app.

What do I need to get started?

Each employer's benefit guide or plan summary (a PDF is fine, even a scanned one), and last year's claims: the explanations of benefits (EOBs) or claims spreadsheet you can download from your insurer's member website under Claims, plus pharmacy claims if you fill prescriptions. A full year of claims gives the best answer. Direct insurer connections are in testing; see insurer connections to request yours.

Does it check whether my doctors are in network?

No. HerePup compares costs, not networks. Before you enroll, check each plan's provider directory for the doctors, hospitals and pharmacies you use.
