Pick yoUr Plan

Choose your health plan with your own claims, not a guess.

HerePup runs last year's real claims through every plan your household can enroll in, across every employer, and shows which choice would have cost you least, once premiums, deductibles, copays and HSA money are counted.

1

Add your plan options

Upload each employer's benefit guide. HerePup reads the plan options, premiums, deductibles and copays, and you confirm every number.

2

Bring in your claims

Upload your explanations of benefits and pharmacy summaries, or connect to your carrier with your own sign-in to pull them directly.

3

See what each plan would cost

Each claim is replayed through every plan, so you see which would have charged a copay and which the full deductible. Then test heavier or lighter years.

Your health data stays with you

HerePup runs on your own computer. Your documents and claims are stored there, not on our servers. We don't have servers that receive them.

  • No HerePup account, and we never see your carrier password
  • Carrier access is read-only and only with your approval
  • Nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising
  • Disconnect or delete everything at any time

Where HerePup is today

HerePup is in private testing. Its estimates come from your own documents and are not a guarantee of cost or a substitute for your plan's documents. Questions or partnership inquiries: chris@herepup.health.
