About HerePup

Every year at open enrollment, households pick a health plan by guessing. HerePup | Pick yoUr Plan replaces the guess with your own claims: it shows what each plan you can choose would really have cost you, and when.

HerePup is a small, independent company. It isn't an insurer or a broker, and it doesn't sell insurance or your information. It's built to run on your own device, so your health information stays with you. HerePup is in early testing, and we'd love to hear how it works for you: chris@herepup.health.

Frequently asked questions

Is HerePup free?

Yes. HerePup is free to use while it's in early testing, and there's no account to create.

Do you see my health information?

No. HerePup runs in your web browser, on your device. Your benefit guides and claims are read there and never uploaded, and HerePup has no servers that receive them. The site only counts anonymous visits and a few steps, with no cookies. You can erase everything with "Delete all my data" in the app.

What do I need to get started?

Each employer's benefit guide or plan summary (a PDF is fine, even a scanned one), and last year's claims: the explanations of benefits (EOBs) or claims spreadsheet you can download from your insurer's member website under Claims, plus pharmacy claims if you fill prescriptions. A full year of claims gives the best answer. See insurer connections for insurers that can share claims directly.

Can it compare plans from two employers?

Yes. If you and a spouse or partner both have employer coverage, add both benefit guides. HerePup tests every way your household can enroll, including splitting people across the two employers' plans.

Does it count employer HSA contributions?

Yes. Money your employer puts into a health savings account (HSA) is subtracted from that plan's cost. You confirm the amounts for each coverage level, can turn the offset off, and if you don't know the amount HerePup shows how the answer changes with it.

How accurate is it?

HerePup replays each claim's allowed amount through each plan's deductibles, coinsurance, copays, prescription tiers and out-of-pocket maximums, and checks itself against what your insurer actually charged when your files include it. It is still an estimate: prices and plan rules change, and specialty drug tiers are not modeled yet.

What if next year looks different?

Use the What if slider to test anywhere from no care to three times last year's care. HerePup shows the points where a different plan would become the better choice.

Does it check whether my doctors are in network?

No. HerePup compares costs, not networks. Before you enroll, check each plan's provider directory for the doctors, hospitals and pharmacies you use.

Is this insurance advice?

No. HerePup doesn't sell insurance and isn't a broker. It gives you an estimate to help you decide; your plan's official documents decide what's covered.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes, in any up-to-date browser. Reading long or scanned PDFs is faster on a computer.
