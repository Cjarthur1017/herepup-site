Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

The short version

  • The HerePup app runs on your own computer. Your documents, claims and carrier data are stored there and are never sent to HerePup.
  • HerePup does not sell, rent or share your information, and does not use it for advertising, marketing or insurance underwriting.
  • The app connects to your health insurer only when you choose to, with your own sign-in at the insurer, and only to read your data.
  • You can disconnect your insurer or delete all of your data in the app at any time.

Who we are

HerePup ("HerePup", "we", "us") makes HerePup | Pick yoUr Plan, an app that helps households compare health plan options using their own claims history. Contact us at privacy@herepup.health.

What the app uses, and why

The app does not use diagnosis information to make its estimates. It reads only what is needed to price claims under each plan.

Where your information is stored

Everything above is stored in the app's data folder on the computer where you run HerePup. HerePup has no servers that receive or store your health information, and we cannot see it. Copies of the data you pull from your insurer are kept in that folder so you can review exactly what was received.

Because your information lives on your device, its security also depends on your device. We recommend using HerePup only on a computer you control, with a password and up-to-date software.

Connecting to your insurer

When you choose to connect an insurer (for example, Aetna), the app sends you to the insurer's own sign-in page. You sign in there and approve read-only access; HerePup never sees or stores your insurer username or password. The insurer then gives the app an access token, which is stored on your computer and used only when you ask the app to pull claims.

You can end this access at any time with Disconnect in the app, which deletes the stored tokens. You may also manage connected apps with your insurer directly. Each adult on a plan connects separately and the app receives only the data the insurer makes available to that person.

Information sent to anyone else

The app sends requests directly from your computer to:

  • Your insurer, only after you connect it, to read your own records.
  • The public NPI Registry run by the U.S. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services, to look up a provider's specialty (for example, whether a visit was with a specialist). Only provider names, and a state if you enter one, are sent; never your name, amounts or diagnoses. You can turn this off in the app.

Nothing else is sent. HerePup does not include advertising, analytics or tracking in the app.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent, license or trade your information.
  • Share it with employers, insurers, brokers, advertisers or data brokers.
  • Use it for advertising, or for underwriting or pricing insurance.
  • Change how your information is used without asking you first.

Deleting your information

"Delete all my data" in the app permanently removes your household, plans, claims, uploaded files, insurer tokens and pulled data from your computer. Removing the app's folder does the same. Because we never receive your information, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

HIPAA

HerePup is a consumer app that you choose to use with your own information. It is not a health plan or health care provider, and it does not act on behalf of one, so HIPAA generally does not apply to information you bring into HerePup. We handle it as described in this policy regardless.

Children

HerePup is meant to be used by adults managing their household's coverage. A household's claims may include children's care; that information is handled the same way as everyone else's and stays on your computer.

This website

herepup.health is a simple informational site hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or tracking. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's privacy statement. If you email us, we use your message only to reply.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above. If a change would affect how your information is used, we will explain it in the app before it takes effect.
