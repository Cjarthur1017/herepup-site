Insurer connections

Updated October 17, 2026

Under a federal rule, many health insurers run a Patient Access API: a secure way for you to sign in on your insurer's own website and let an app read your claims. HerePup can use these connections to bring in your claims without downloading files. Each insurer must first approve HerePup as an app.

Will it work for my plan?

The rule covers Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, CHIP and ACA marketplace plans. It does not require employer plans to take part, and many employer plans are run by the employer (self-funded) even when an insurer's name is on the card. Some insurers include employer plans anyway. Don't see your insurer, or not approved yet? You don't need a connection to use HerePup. Sign in to your insurer's member website, open Claims, and download a claims spreadsheet or your explanation of benefits (EOB) PDFs. Then drag them into HerePup. They're read on your device and never uploaded.

How a connection works

  1. You choose your insurer in HerePup and are sent to your insurer's own sign-in page. HerePup never sees your password.
  2. You approve read-only access to your claims and coverage.
  3. HerePup reads your claims and keeps them on your device. You can disconnect at any time.

Insurer connections are available in the HerePup desktop app today and come to the web version as insurers approve HerePup. See our privacy policy for details.
