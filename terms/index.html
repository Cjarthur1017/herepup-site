What HerePup is

HerePup | Pick yoUr Plan is a tool that estimates what different health plans are likely to cost your household, using a model built from the plan documents and claims history you provide. It is currently offered in early testing. HerePup is operated by Christopher Arthur ("HerePup", "we", "us").

Estimates, not advice or guarantees

HerePup's results are estimates based on your documents, the numbers you confirm, and assumptions the app shows you. They are not a promise of what any plan will cost, and they are not medical, insurance, financial, tax or legal advice. Plan rules, provider networks and prices change, and your future care may differ from your past claims. Check important decisions against your plan's official documents, and with your employer or insurer.

Your responsibilities

  • Use HerePup only with information about yourself and people in your household whom you are authorized to act for, and connect only your own insurer account.
  • HerePup is for adults. It is not directed to children under 13.
  • Review the values HerePup reads from your documents before relying on them.
  • Keep the device where you use HerePup secure, since your information is stored there.
  • Follow your insurer's terms when you connect HerePup to your insurer account.

Insurer connections

When you confirm and connect an insurer, you are authorizing HerePup to read your records from that insurer on your behalf, as described in the Privacy Policy. The insurer controls what data is available and may limit, change or end access. You can disconnect at any time.

Privacy
