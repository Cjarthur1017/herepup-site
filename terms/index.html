What HerePup is

HerePup | Pick yoUr Plan is a tool that estimates what different health plans would have cost your household, using plan documents and claims you provide. It is currently offered in private testing.

Estimates, not advice or guarantees

HerePup's results are estimates based on your documents, the numbers you confirm, and assumptions the app shows you. They are not a promise of what any plan will cost, and they are not medical, insurance, financial, tax or legal advice. Plan rules, provider networks and prices change, and your future care may differ from your past claims. Check important decisions against your plan's official documents, and with your employer or insurer.

Your responsibilities

  • Use HerePup only with information about yourself and people in your household whom you are authorized to act for.
  • Review the values HerePup reads from your documents before relying on them.
  • Keep the computer where you run HerePup secure, since your information is stored there.
  • Follow your insurer's terms when you connect HerePup to your insurer account.

Insurer connections

When you connect an insurer, you are authorizing HerePup to read your records from that insurer on your behalf. The insurer controls what data is available and may limit, change or end access. You can disconnect at any time.

Privacy

Our Privacy Policy explains how information is handled. In short, your health information stays on your computer and is never sold or shared.
